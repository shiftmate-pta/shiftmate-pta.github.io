ShiftMate 3.1.19 payroll rule audit
Basis: full-time metropolitan Transit Officers, with user-confirmed roster,
classification, employment circumstances and payroll declarations. The WAIRC
register lists the 2023 agreement in force. Confirm any later written roster
arrangements or employer directives before relying on affected estimates.

Ordinary pay and weekends
  Clauses 1.6.4, 3.1.1, 3.1.3, 3.1.7 and 4.1.1; Schedule 1. Use
  the effective classification's weekly base divided by 40 for full-time
  ordinary hours. Split at midnight, with 150% Saturday and 200% Sunday
  defaults in place of other shift penalties. Manual rates and multipliers
  require a verified basis. Coverage: payCalculator and annualLeaveLoading
  unit suites and five-period replay.

Overtime and higher duties
  Clauses 3.2.1–3.2.6. Use posted ordinary hours and separately entered
  additional shifts. Ordinary Transit Officer overtime is 184% on weekdays
  and 200% on weekends under standard settings. Holiday work uses clause 6.1.
  Friday/Saturday built-in overtime inferred from operational shift codes
  requires the applicable posted roster or written arrangement. Callback
  minima under clause 3.2.7 and insufficient rest under clause 3.4.3 are
  not established by the generic overtime flag.
  Clause 2.9 requires the assigned higher-position rate and substantial,
  unshared responsibilities. The fixed standard uplift is provisional.
  Acting Transit Supervisor is a separate, explicitly selected estimate.
  The configurable rate and its effective date remain supported. The
  observed default of $50.89 and 150%/200% split cover weekday picked-up
  overtime through 10 hours only. Holiday or weekend segments and longer
  shifts use the standard model with a warning. No supervisor instrument
  was available to confirm the rates. Coverage: payrollCorrections,
  payCalculator, rosterProjection and browser upgrade.

Shift allowances and training
  Clauses 3.1.3, 3.1.7(c), 5.1.1–5.1.6 and 1.6.13. Use ordinary
  roster time to classify early morning (04:00–05:30), afternoon (start
  before 18:00 and ordinary finish from 18:30), or night (18:00–03:59 start).
  The separate per-shift amount applies to qualifying ordinary endpoints
  01:01–03:59, subject to the weekend rule. Rates are 13.5% and 16% of the
  hourly reference rate, with the latter also used for the per-shift amount.
  October $4.87 afternoon and $5.77 additional allowance inputs were
  corroborated in payroll records; the current PTA rate notice was not
  available. Station fractional-hour rounding remains a payroll convention
  pending a directive. Training and administration use worked hours for
  the allowance, excluding booked-off leave.
  Clause 4.3 pays base plus 15% for employer-directed supplementary
  training after initial training. The weekday loading is applied to
  worked ordinary hours, separately from the section 5.1 shift allowance.
  The interaction with weekend penalties and additional hours remains
  unresolved. Coverage: trainingAdmin unit/browser and payCalculator.

Travel and vehicle payments
  Clauses 5.2.1–5.2.5, 3.5.8(d), and Schedule 2. Directed off-line work
  pays one base hour plus return kilometres, subject to eligibility and
  cost-neutral roster rules. Employer-directed supplementary training
  outside designated lines uses clause 5.2.5 for non-trainees.
  Guildford is 22.6 km return from the Armadale home-line reference station
  in Schedule 2. Use 98 cents/km for voluntary metropolitan vehicle use
  from 1 July 2026 under Circular 6/2026. Armadale SAFEK 80.8 km and Perth
  10.7 km are payroll-evidenced route overrides, with the SAFEK manual
  distance still taking precedence. No inference of formal Schedule 2
  amendment is made. Coverage: trainingAdmin and offlineAllowanceCoverage.

Leave and public holidays
  Clauses 6.3, 6.5 and 6.5.7 with the August 2026 PTA/RTBU arrangement
  already modelled by LIVE. Pay approved leave hours at the applicable
  base; use the substantive classification and date-effective loading
  and annual cap. Prior loading history and future cap data may be needed.
  For partial book-offs preserve the posted shift, separate worked time
  from approved leave, and apply loading only to eligible annual leave.
  Clause 6.2 ADL is approved Easter Sunday additional leave at base rate,
  available from Easter Sunday within that calendar year. A roster ADL
  entry assumes employer approval and available entitlement; the app does
  not administer leave balances or employment eligibility.
  Clauses 6.1.2–6.1.9 govern substitution, worked holiday pay and
  approved leave. Split work by calendar day. Pay 150% worked holiday
  hours, then apply the one cash or agreed leave-in-lieu election and
  applicable minimum. Conflicting elections show worked pay only and a
  warning. Check any statutory or employer substitution and unusual
  minimum/cap cases separately. Coverage: annualLeaveLoading,
  payCalculator, payrollCorrections, trainingAdmin and upgrade tests.

PAYG and deductions
  Use payment date, resident scale 1 or 2, and Schedule 1 coefficients.
  Divide fortnightly taxable earnings by two, discard cents and add
  99 cents, apply a*x-b, round to the nearest dollar once, then double.
  2024 and 2025 instruments have identical supported coefficients; the
  2026 instrument applies from 1 July 2026. Before July 2024 the app
  labels its legacy annualised estimate. Ordinary wages, overtime,
  penalties, leave/loading and taxable allowances enter the withholding
  base. Effective pre-tax arrangements reduce that base; configured
  additional withholding increases PAYG; post-tax deductions reduce
  cash net only. Other scales, Medicare adjustments, study/training
  loans, tax offsets and special back-payment methods require separate
  assessment. No additional withholding is inferred from a payslip.
  The mileage component is still excluded in the estimate and flagged.
  A qualifying private-car cents/km allowance generally requires
  withholding on the rate excess for up to 5,000 business kilometres and
  on all amounts above the limit. The 2026–27 approved rate is 91 cents.
  Reimbursements of actual expense and full-withholding allowances have
  different treatments. The payroll/STP classification, trip conditions
  and cumulative kilometres must be established. Gross includes mileage
  once for reconciliation; withholding treatment is separate from
  year-end assessability. Coverage: payrollCorrections Schedule 1 sample
  rows, effective-date boundary and deductions tests; five-period
  independent calculation and same-origin upgrade.
